# Hi there 👋

I'm Simon, a Norwegian student, triathlete, and aspiring mechatronics & aerospace engineer.

I build software, AI systems, engineering tools, and experimental hardware — mostly as part of **Draconis Engineering**, my growing engineering and software ecosystem.

My goal is simple: **learn by building real things.**

I’m especially interested in the intersection of software, AI, robotics, high-performance computing, and physical engineering.

---

## ⚙️ Draconis Engineering

**Draconis Engineering** is an independent engineering and software ecosystem built around the idea that ambitious projects become more powerful when their tools, infrastructure, and ideas are connected.

Some of the projects I'm currently developing:

### 🏛️ Olympus

**My flagship project.**

A high-performance training and analytics ecosystem for triathlon and endurance sports.

Currently focused on **Olympus Terminal**, a Rust-based TUI cycling trainer, with an analytics engine and additional training tools planned.

Olympus is also becoming one of the first major applications built on top of Draconis' numerical infrastructure.

### ⚡ DracoLIX

**The numerical backbone of Draconis.**

A high-performance computing and numerical mathematics platform designed to provide the computational foundation for Draconis' engineering and scientific software.

The current direction is a **C++ core** with interfaces for languages such as Python, Julia, and Rust, alongside lower-level HPC and accelerator technologies.

### 🐍 DuraPy

**The Draconis Engineering Python Collection.**

A growing collection of Python tools and libraries for mathematics, physics, robotics, engineering, and scientific computing.

Designed to make Draconis' lower-level systems accessible from Python.

### 📊 DragonQuant

**Economic simulation & modelling.**

A planned simulation platform for economics, finance, and complex systems, powered by Draconis' numerical infrastructure.

### 🧪 Draconomicon

**Engineering & physics simulation.**

A planned simulation platform exploring areas such as computational fluid dynamics, plasma physics, energy systems, and other computational engineering problems.

### DraconiForge

**DevOps Utilities*

A simple, lightweight, but also powerful CLI utility that serves to make CI/CD easier via Git streamlining, project templates, and more.

## 🧠 What I'm Learning

I'm deliberately exploring several engineering disciplines before deciding where I want to specialize.

* **Software:** Python, Rust, C, C++, Go, Shell
* **HPC & Scientific Computing:** C++, Fortran, Julia, numerical methods
* **AI & ML:** LLMs, neural networks, inference, optimization, AI systems
* **Robotics:** embedded systems, control theory, ROS, mechatronics
* **Engineering:** mechanical, aerospace, electrical & electronics engineering
* **Digital Fabrication:** CAD, 3D printing, PCB design, KiCad & FreeCAD

I'm particularly interested in understanding systems from the **lowest level to the highest level** — from hardware and numerical computation all the way to the software people actually use.

## 🎯 Why I Build

I've always been fascinated by intelligent machines, aerospace technology, and ambitious engineering projects.

Instead of waiting until university to start learning, I use personal projects as laboratories.

I build something → encounter a problem → learn what I need → rebuild it better.

Some projects fail. Some get abandoned. Others turn into something much bigger than I originally expected.

That's part of the process.

**Every project is another step toward becoming the engineer I want to be.**
